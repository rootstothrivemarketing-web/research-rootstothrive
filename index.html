About This Subdomain

research.rootstothrive.com is an official subdomain of Roots to Thrive Society for Psychedelic Therapy, used for our research program correspondence and activities.

Our Mission

The research initiative of the Roots to Thrive Society for Psychedelic Therapy is dedicated to advancing the scientific understanding, safety, and accessibility of psychedelic-assisted treatments. Through rigorous academic collaboration, evidence-based evaluation, and community-centered inquiry, we seek to validate innovative therapeutic models that foster deep healing, resilience, and systemic well-being for individuals and communities navigating complex mental health challenges.
